# Operator Practice Problems

Solve these problems without looking at the solutions.

## Basic

- Add two numbers.
- Find the remainder.
- Swap two numbers without a third variable.
- Convert Celsius to Fahrenheit.
- Calculate simple interest.

## Intermediate

- Check whether a student passed.
- Find the maximum of three numbers.
- Check voting eligibility.
- Calculate employee salary with bonus.
- Grade calculator.

## Advanced

- Login validation using username and password.
- Electricity bill calculation.
- Income tax calculator.
- BMI calculator.
- ATM withdrawal validation.
